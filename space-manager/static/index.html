House rules

Where things should live

SSD C:
Windows, apps, games you play (Valorant, Genshin, Minecraft, Steam), active projects and reels you're still editing.
HDD D:
Photos, videos, clips, finished reel frames, backups and archives. It follows D:\README.md: unsure? Put it in 00_Inbox.
USB
D: is a USB exFAT drive. Eject it before unplugging, and keep a second copy of anything you can't lose.
