Hook up your phone

  1. Install Beam 1.4.0 on the phone (QT Suite › APKs).
  2. Open Beam on the phone and scan this PC's QR code (once).
  3. On the Files tab, find the Notifications to PC card and tap Turn on. Android asks you to allow notification access for Beam.
  4. Tap Keep running so Android doesn't put Beam to sleep.

Works on home Wi-Fi, and anywhere with Tailscale on. Nothing leaves your PC and phone.
